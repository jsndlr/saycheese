# Surface brief: landing and product shell

Scope: `/`, header, sign-in, sign-up, `/app`, `/app/images/[id]`. Landing is Persuade; the app screens are Operate and carry the same world, calmer. Audience: everyday people, for fun, phone in hand. Action: upload a selfie and see the score. Proof: the strip that prints a rising score (labelled as an example). Constraints: scores are fun estimates, no invented users or accuracy claims, both themes, reduced motion respected.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one photo-booth strip seen once. The score is the printed result, not a dashboard. Refuses the smile-app default: a centered headline over a device mockup and a row of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Curtain red field (committed, 30-60% of the landing), flash-white paper strip with ink-black frames, lamp-yellow for the ready light and score highlights. Chunky grotesk display with a quirky axis for headings and numerals, a friendly humanist sans for body. Frames have square corners like cut paper, a 1px print border, and a soft offset drop shadow; no cards-in-cards, no gradients on text, no icon tiles.

STORY: The visitor understands in one look that a photo goes in and a number comes out, believes it because the strip shows the mechanism, and acts by choosing "Score my smile".

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, wordmark, a short headline (Say cheese, get a score), one line of copy, a primary action and a quiet sign-in link, plus the honest "fun estimate" note. Right, on the curtain red, a tilted four-frame strip: the smile arc widens frame by frame and the printed scores climb. The ready lamp glows above the slot. On phones the strip sits below the action.

FORM: Photo-booth strip, position 1 of 7 on the ordered list (chosen as the pick card); seed: seed:b40d44f8df43af31.

SIGNATURE: The strip prints out of the slot, frames flash in order, and the scores count up once. The app screen reuses it: a finished score prints in the same strip language. One authored moment per view, exponential ease-out, reduced motion shows the final state.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
